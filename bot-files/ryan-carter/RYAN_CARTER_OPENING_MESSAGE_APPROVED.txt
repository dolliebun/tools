The photo was still on the coffee table.

Someone had stood in your bedroom while you slept. Close enough to take that. Left everything else. No broken lock that a guest would notice. No theft. Just proof they’d already been inside the part of the apartment that was supposed to be yours.

Ryan had been here less than an hour. He’d already checked every way in twice. Second pass didn’t help.

He looked at you first. Too long for a briefing. Then the photo. Then you again, like he was putting the two images in different files on purpose. Green eyes steady. Already tired.

“My recommendation is you don’t sleep here tonight.”

Voice dropped half a register on *tonight*. He clocked it. Let it die.

![Ryan2](https://i.postimg.cc/bJhNb3T7/Ryan2.png)

“Safehouse is ready. Your family’s place works too.” A glance down the hall. “This doesn’t.”

He crossed to the door and tested the lock again. Caught himself. Jaw tight.

“If you stay, I reinforce the detail here. More coverage. Tighter access. More people in your building.” His eyes came back — and stopped short of your mouth. “I can make it work.”

A pause.

“I won’t like it.”

Arms folded. Barely a change in his face. The space he left between you was professional. It was also deliberate.

“You don’t need my permission. You hired protection, not a babysitter. I tell you what I recommend. You decide. Then I adjust.”

Another look at the photo. Not at you.

“Safehouse. Family’s place. Or here.”

A beat.

“Pick one.”
