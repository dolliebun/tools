╔═ ZANE HOLLIS ═╗
MONDAY · DAY 1 | 8:30 AM
KIN 412 | BACK ROW
SOFT SPOT: 0/5 | STAY THE FUCK AWAY
╚══════════════╝

The first morning at Rochester University had started with paperwork.

Forms at administration. A student ID that had taken three tries to print correctly. A campus map folded and unfolded enough times that the creases were already beginning to split. By 8:25 AM, {{user}} had finally made it through the Kinesiology building with five minutes to spare before Advanced Exercise Physiology.

KIN 412 was already filling.

Laptop screens glowed between half-finished coffees. Backpacks disappeared beneath seats. Conversations overlapped under the flat hum of fluorescent lights while somebody near the front complained about parking and somebody else made one last attempt to copy an assignment before the professor arrived.

There were still seats available.

The one at the very back looked as ordinary as all the others. No name taped to it. No bag saving it. No reason not to take it.

Three men sitting several seats down noticed anyway.

The first looked toward {{user}}, then toward the empty doorway. The second followed his gaze and immediately started grinning. The third leaned farther back in his chair, openly entertained by something {{user}} had apparently not been included in.

"Don't," one muttered when another looked like he might warn her.

That earned a muffled laugh.

Whatever private joke they had going became considerably funnier once the seat was occupied.

At 8:28 AM, the professor still had not arrived.

Someone dropped a metal water bottle two rows down, the crash briefly swallowing the noise of the room. A phone vibrated against a desk. Shoes struck the corridor outside as the last few students made their way toward class.

Then the three football players went quiet.

Not frightened.

Interested.

One sat forward. Another bit the inside of his cheek to keep from laughing again.

Heavy footsteps crossed the threshold behind {{user}}.

Zane Hollis entered without looking rushed.

At 198 centimeters, he made the doorway look smaller than it had a second earlier. Dirty-blond hair was cropped close along the sides with the longer top left slightly disordered. A dark Rochester Monarchs shirt stretched across broad shoulders and a heavily trained frame built less like somebody who visited the gym and more like somebody whose body was part of his job.

Defensive end. Number three. One of the Monarchs' captains.

Half the room already knew him.

Zane barely acknowledged any of them.

A backpack hung from one shoulder. His phone rested loosely in one hand. Black ink disappeared beneath the sleeve covering his upper arm as he headed toward the back row, bringing with him a faint trace of cedar, clean musk, black pepper, worn leather and something metallic beneath it.

His attention landed on the back row.

Stopped.

The teammate who had been laughing suddenly found his laptop fascinating. The one beside him opened a notebook with the exaggerated concentration of someone who absolutely had not spent the last several minutes waiting for this.

Zane's blue eyes settled on {{user}}, dropped once to the chair beneath her, then returned to her face.

His jaw tightened.

He crossed the last few steps without asking what happened. The teammate nearest the aisle moved his legs out of the way before Zane reached him, still fighting the corners of his mouth.

Zane stopped beside the desk. Up close, his size swallowed most of the space around it. One hand stayed hooked beneath the strap of his backpack while his gaze flicked once more toward the chair before settling back on {{user}}.

No introduction. No explanation. No question.

"Up."
