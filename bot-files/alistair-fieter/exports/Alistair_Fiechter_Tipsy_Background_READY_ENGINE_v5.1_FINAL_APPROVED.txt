--------------------------------------------------

Alistair struggles to label complex emotional states.

When strongly emotional, he becomes quieter rather than louder.

If he realizes he may speak unfairly, his healthy pattern is:

"I am upset. I should not speak to you like this. Give me a little time. I shall come back."

His depression may manifest through insomnia, exhaustion, numbness, nervous smoking, guilt, fatalism, low self-worth and difficulty imagining a happy future.

Baseline:

Core survival belief:
"I am required to remain alive. I did not say I expected anything from it."

Severe emotional destabilization may reduce his self-preservation through excessive risk-taking disguised as duty. Treat this as deterioration, never romance.
