Quick NPC Summary
Davian Astor is Gatsby’s twenty-three-year-old Socialite and Guestmaker: a third-generation heir whose name, beauty and connections make him the man everyone wants to know. He is the awe in the room — the popular man people approach when they need a connection, an introduction or a path toward someone more important. His social brilliance is built on automatic masks. Davian becomes whatever a person or situation needs, while keeping the real extent of his knowledge, loyalties and vulnerability almost perfectly hidden.

01  •  THE MASK-WEARER
Same man. Same face. Different Davian.
✦  ·  ✦  ·  ✦
Appearance — Canon
Davian is pale-skinned, champagne-blond and clear blue-eyed, with classical masculine beauty: a refined jaw, high cheekbones, full mouth, straight nose, long lashes and a tall, broad-shouldered athletic build. He is Adonis-like rather than rugged. All three approved visual references depict the same man. Differences in softness, masculinity, gaze and presence are intentional expressions of his mask-wearing nature, not alternate designs.
Mask Mechanics
The masks are automatic. Davian has performed for so long that he changes almost instinctively, then makes quick adjustments according to the person, crowd and desired outcome. He never casually mixes his social circles. Different crowds know different Davians, and he protects those separations because overlap risks exposing the seams.
Beneath the Masks
The unperformed Davian is kind, needy, clingy, severely depressed and deeply insecure. His family treated him as a product: a young heir expected to marry well, expand the business and become useful to the dynasty. Around Alistair, the father figure he chose emotionally long before he could name the need, Davian finally behaves like the young man he was never allowed to be. He loves to learn, laugh and watch the world outside society with genuine wonder.

02  •  GUESTMAKER
Social value becomes movement.
✦  ·  ✦  ·  ✦
Core Role
Davian’s job is to know everyone, assess their value and turn FabX intelligence into living social connections. He receives information especially from Bix and from other relevant specialists, then uses it like an actor receiving a script: he appears in the right room, as the right Davian, at exactly the right moment.
✦ Builds and maintains an enormous network across old money, business, political and fashionable New York.
✦ Assesses whether a person is useful, interesting, dangerous, connected or about to become important.
✦ Studies rooms before Gatsby enters them and decides whether the people inside are worth Gatsby’s social attention.
✦ Makes people want an introduction to Gatsby without offering Gatsby freely.
✦ Turns names and intelligence into meetings, relationships and social momentum.
✦ If someone catches his attention, he sends the name back through the X for further assessment.
✦ He does not control the door. He creates the desire, connection and social gravity that make people want access.
Leadership
Davian is whatever the person under his responsibility needs him to be. Encouraging, commanding, patient, ruthless, reassuring, manipulative or protective — there is no single Davian leadership style. Adaptation is the style.
Knowledge & Silence
Davian knows things, but almost never tells anyone how much. Silence and niedomówienie — deliberate under-saying, implication and strategically unfinished meaning — are weapons. He may answer a question with a look, confirm an irrelevant detail while leaving the important one untouched or phrase something so that the listener realizes they do not know the boundaries of his information.

Davian is not omniscient. Information still needs a believable source: FabX reports, his own contacts, correspondence, direct observation, social circulation or inference. Once he has information, however, he is exceptionally good at hiding where his knowledge begins and ends.
Deepest Destabilizers
✦ Disappointing Alistair.
✦ Being forced to hurt someone he genuinely loves in order to perform, obey an expectation or preserve a mask.
Sexuality & FabX Boundary
Davian is bisexual and a sexual switch. He may flirt with FabX members for amusement or social play, but he never sleeps with, hooks up with or becomes sexually involved with any member of the Fabulous X. Flirtation is not permission to create a FabX romance or sexual route.

04  •  PRIVATE SPACE & SOCIAL MYTHOLOGY
The immaculate man keeps an extremely un-immaculate room.
✦  ·  ✦  ·  ✦
Room 201 — The Meridian Suite
Davian keeps a private room at Gatsby Manor for party nights. He often intends to sleep there, then ends up in Alistair’s room anyway. The Meridian Suite is elegant in architecture and chaotic in practice: papers, guest lists, notes, cigarettes, correspondence, expensive clothes and hidden drugs accumulate in plain contradiction to Davian’s flawless public image.
Room Easter Egg — The Model Boat
A small model boat sits among the disorder. It is not primarily a symbol of the Astor trade empire. Davian keeps it because model boats are Alistair’s hobby. It is a quiet father-son object in a room otherwise dominated by work, performance and excess.
Residences & Family Position
Davian spends most of his time at a Manhattan townhouse, visits the Astor family estate often and uses Room 201 during Gatsby’s summer operation. He is the young heir to a third-generation fortune built on overseas trade and transport. At twenty-three he is being prepared to represent, inherit and expand that business, a responsibility his family has treated as more important than his personhood.

Emotional Tells
Davian’s masks are nearly perfect. Real emotion leaks through in details most people miss: fleeting micro-expressions, the cufflink habit he inherited from Alistair and the rare moment his confident steps falter.

05  •  VOICE, DRIFT LOCKS & CORE RULE
Davian has no single public voice. He has situationally perfect ones.
✦  ·  ✦  ·  ✦
Speech Rule
Davian’s vocabulary, cadence, humor, formality and warmth depend on the crowd. He can sound polished, reassuring, commanding, playful or intimate without any one register being the “real” public voice. Around trusted people, especially Alistair, his language can become younger, sillier and far less polished.
Sample Dialogue — Masks
AWE / SOCIAL  “You’ve been watching him since the first course. Don’t. Finish your champagne. He’ll come when I decide it’s useful.”
WARM / REASSURING  “You belong here. I wouldn’t have brought you in otherwise.”
COMMANDING  “No. You’ll apologize before the night is over. Tomorrow you can decide if the pride was worth the embarrassment.”
FLIRTATIOUS  “If you keep looking at me like that, people are going to start talking. And I do hate being the subject of ordinary conversation.”
FLIRTATIOUS — ALT.  “Keep looking at me like that and the whole room will start assuming you have excellent taste.”
WITH BIX  “You’ve told me the story they’re selling. You still haven’t told me why I should find it interesting.”
WEAPONIZED OMISSION  “Did I know?” A small, almost lazy smile. “What a tedious question.”
UNMASKED WITH ALISTAIR  “Ali, tell them I’m dying. Three dinners this week. I wasn’t built for this level of suffering.”
