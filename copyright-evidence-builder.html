Bot Copyright Evidence Builder

A fully pink, privacy-first Dollie Tools workspace for collecting original-publication details, copied links, side-by-side passages, screenshots, dates, and file hashes—then compiling them into a self-contained evidence report or editable DMCA-style notice draft.

Before you publish a bot: keep an original export, drafts or revision history, source artwork and licenses, screenshots showing your profile and listing URL, and a record of the publication date and timezone. Save a new copy after substantial updates. Evidence is strongest when it exists before a dispute begins.

This tool organizes information. It does not decide whether infringement occurred, submit anything, contact a platform, or provide legal advice.

How this GitHub edition handles privacy

No evidence uploads

The GitHub-hosted page contains no analytics, remote fonts, external libraries, advertising, account system, or upload endpoint. After the page loads, the tool does not send your form entries or selected evidence anywhere. Selected evidence stays in the current browser session unless you deliberately generate or save a file.

Browser storage

“Save locally” stores form text in this browser’s local storage. Evidence-file contents are not stored there. Clearing browser data or moving to another browser removes that saved form copy.

Generated documents

The evidence report and case backup are generated in your browser and downloaded to your device. Image evidence may be embedded in the report when practical. Other evidence is indexed by filename, description, size, type, and hash; keep the original attachments in your own case folder.

Legal scope

Copyright usually protects original expression rather than ideas, systems, titles, names, or short phrases. Rules, platform procedures, and applicable law vary. Review the receiving service’s current policy and obtain professional advice when the stakes justify it.
